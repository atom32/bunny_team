# Poly Haven streets textures

Downloaded original 1K JPG diffuse, OpenGL normal and roughness maps on 2026-10-01.

- Rob Tuytel: https://polyhaven.com/a/asphalt_02 (3 m tile) and https://polyhaven.com/a/wall_bricks_plaster (1 m tile).
- Amal Kumar: https://polyhaven.com/a/painted_plaster_wall (2 m tile).
- License: CC0, https://polyhaven.com/license

Original bytes, exact download URLs and SHA256 hashes are recorded in checksums.json. All nine maps use mipmaps; normal maps are explicitly imported as normals. Used by the original Old Town district; no Tarkov assets.
