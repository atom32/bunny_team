# Campus surface assets

2026-10-08. Unmodified original 1K JPG files from [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com), [CC0](https://polyhaven.com/license).

- Charlotte Baglioni: [Leafy Grass](https://polyhaven.com/a/leafy_grass), 2 m source tile, used at a restrained 4 m presentation scale.
- Rob Tuytel: [Roof Tiles 14](https://polyhaven.com/a/roof_tiles_14), weathered ceramic roof tiles. Roof shader changes material tint and saturation; source pixels are unchanged.
- Jan Burghardt: [Pavement 05](https://polyhaven.com/a/pavement_05), 3.4 m stone paving tile.

Exact download URLs and SHA-256 checksums are in checksums.json. Diffuse, OpenGL normal and roughness maps are included. The public Tianjin University campus map was used as a layout reference, not shipped as a game asset.

- Rob Tuytel (photography/processing), Rico Cilliers (minor adjustment): [Forest Ground 04](https://polyhaven.com/a/forest_ground_04), CC0; 1K diffuse map, verified against publisher API MD5. Used for feathered planting soil and existing tree-root litter, not new collision or terrain height.
